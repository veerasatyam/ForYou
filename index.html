🌸 About the Simulation

Pointillism Lily computes and renders over 15,000 distinct particle points every frame. It simulates botanical growth phases through smooth step timing interpolations, parametric 3D space midrib curves, dynamic wind sway, and stamen anther placement.

The bloom's glow effect is achieved using real-time additive dual-pass scaling, illuminating individual petals as they emerge from the base soil mound into full radiance.
